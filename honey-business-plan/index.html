PART 1One-page summary · የአንድ ገጽ ማጠቃለያ

Hive & Wax Works: buy fairly, process cleanly, sell with a name

What: a small honey and beeswax processing business. It buys crude honey from beekeepers, strains and settles it without heat, tests the moisture, and sells it in labelled jars that show the place of origin. Wax that beekeepers used to throw away is melted in a solar box and sold as clean blocks or made into products.

Why honey: it scored first of ten opportunities in Part 2. Raw material is everywhere, farm-gate honey sells for about half the Addis Ababa price, wax has jumped to over 1,500 birr/kg, and all the tools can be built locally.

Where: in a honey area with an open road to a town market, for example Masha, Bonga, Jimma, or Debre Markos when roads there are safe again. Sell first in your town, then in Addis Ababa and Hawassa.

Who: one founder plus one helper in year 1; beekeeper suppliers paid fairly and on time.

Growth: later sell modern hives and wax foundation sheets to the same beekeepers, buy back their bigger harvests, and add a hive-scale sensor service.

The name "Hive & Wax Works" is an example: use your own. All prices are November 2026 estimates (about 162 birr per US dollar). Get three quotes for every item before you buy.

PART 2Problem, customers and edge · ችግር፣ ደንበኞችና ብልጫ

The problem you solve, and who pays you

The problem. Beekeepers sell crude honey for less than 400 birr/kg because they have no direct route to market, while the same honey fetches up to 800 birr/kg in Addis Ababa. Most Ethiopian honey is sold unbranded, much of it to tej makers, and the country has no internationally accredited honey-testing laboratory. Buyers cannot easily tell good honey from watered or adulterated honey.

Your customers.

  • Shops and supermarkets: 250 g, 500 g and 1 kg jars.
  • Hotels, cafés and bakeries: 5 kg tubs at a wholesale price.
  • Tej houses: strained honey in bulk; they buy most of the honey sold in Ethiopia.
  • Gift and diaspora buyers: single-origin jars sold online by Telegram, TikTok and WhatsApp.
  • Soap, cosmetic and candle makers: clean wax blocks.
  • Beekeepers (from year 2): hives and wax foundation sheets.

Your edge. Every jar shows the place, the harvest month, a lot number and the measured water content. You never heat the honey. You pay beekeepers a posted price, above the farm-gate price, on the day of delivery.

PART 3Operations and engineering · አሠራርና ምሕንድስና

How it works, and the equipment you build

  1. Buy and test. Weigh, smell and taste each delivery and check a sample with the refractometer. Reject honey above 20% water as table honey; it can still go to tej makers.
  2. Strain. Coarse mesh, then fine mesh, into food-grade buckets. Press the comb with the screw press.
  3. Settle. 2–3 days at room temperature in the tank. Bubbles and wax rise.
  4. Check and pack. Measure the water again, fill jars from the bottom tap, label with place, month, lot number and water %.
  5. Wax line. Pressed comb and cappings go into the solar melter, through a mesh, into moulds. Clean blocks are weighed and stored.
Build it

Settling tank. 100 L stainless steel, with a sloping bottom and a honey gate (tap) 3 cm above the base so sediment stays behind. A local metal workshop can make it; insist on food-grade stainless steel and smooth welds.

Solar wax melter. A wooden box about 100 × 60 cm, 5 cm of insulation, a black-painted metal tray, a double-glass lid, set at about 30° facing the sun. 70–90 °C inside on a clear day.

Year 3: hive-scale sensor. A 200 kg load cell under the hive, an HX711 amplifier, an ESP32 board, and a SIM800L module that sends one SMS a day; powered by a 6 V solar panel and an 18650 battery. Weight going up means nectar is flowing, so harvest at the right time. A sudden drop means a swarm or theft. Parts cost an estimated 5,000–8,000 birr.

Quality standard you work to
  • Water ≤ 20% (Codex Alimentarius honey standard).
  • HMF ≤ 40 mg/kg: never heat above 40 °C.
  • Ethiopian standards for honey (ES 1202), beeswax (ES 1203) and beehives (ES 1204), first published in 2005 and revised in 2025.
  • Send one sample per season to a lab for HMF and adulteration tests; keep the report for buyers.

የፀሐይ ሰም ማቅለጫ። 100 × 60 ሳ.ሜ ገደማ የእንጨት ሳጥን፣ 5 ሳ.ሜ ሙቀት መከላከያ፣ ጥቁር ቀለም የተቀባ የብረት ትሪ፣ ድርብ መስታወት ክዳን፣ ወደ ፀሐይ በ30° ገደማ ያዘነበለ። ጥርት ባለ ቀን ውስጡ 70–90 °C።

ዓመት 3፦ የቀፎ ሚዛን ዳሳሽ። ከቀፎው ሥር 200 ኪግ የሚመዝን ሎድ ሴል፣ HX711 ማጉያ፣ ESP32 ቦርድ፣ በቀን አንድ SMS የሚልክ SIM800L፤ በ6 V የፀሐይ ፓነልና በ18650 ባትሪ የሚሠራ። ክብደቱ ሲጨምር የአበባ ማር እየገባ ነው፣ ስለዚህ በትክክለኛው ጊዜ ይቁረጡ። ድንገት ሲቀንስ የንብ መንጋ ወጥቷል ወይም ተሰርቋል። ዕቃዎቹ ከ5,000–8,000 ብር ገደማ ይገመታሉ።

PART 4Start-up budget · የመነሻ በጀት

What it costs to start: standard or lean

The standard plan starts with 300 kg of honey a month and a proper workshop. The lean plan starts with 100 kg, buckets instead of a tank, no press, and a smaller room, and grows from its own profit. The biggest item in both is the first stock of honey: that money comes back as soon as you sell.

Equipment prices are estimates. Ask three suppliers for each item, and build what you can yourself.

ለመጀመር የሚያስፈልገው ወጪ፦ መደበኛ ወይም ቀላል

PART 7Registration and funding · ምዝገባና ፋይናንስ

Make it legal, then fund it carefully

Registration and licensing are now done online, and in one recent three-month period Ethiopia delivered over a million registration and licensing services digitally. Use it.

Funding, in this order: your own savings and family; pre-orders and deposits from shops and hotels; supplier credit from beekeepers who trust you; then a loan from a microfinance institution or a bank's youth or small-business window. Ask your woreda small-enterprise office which programmes are open. Borrow only after your first three batches have shown real margins.

    የግብር ከፋይ መለያ ቁጥር ያውጡ።

  2. Trade name and registration

    Reserve the name and register on the e-Trade portal (etrade.gov.et).

    የንግድ ስምና ምዝገባ

    ስሙን ያስይዙና በኢ-ትሬድ ፖርታል (etrade.gov.et) ይመዝገቡ።

  3. Premises

    A rented or owned room with a lease; clean, washable, screened.

    የሥራ ቦታ

    በውል የተከራዩት ወይም የራስዎ ክፍል፤ ንጹሕ፣ የሚታጠብ፣ የዝንብ መከላከያ ያለው።

  4. Certificate of competence

    Food processing needs one from the sector regulator before the licence. Ask the woreda trade office which office inspects you.

    የብቃት ማረጋገጫ

    የምግብ ማቀነባበር ከፈቃዱ በፊት ከዘርፉ ተቆጣጣሪ ማረጋገጫ ይፈልጋል። የሚመረምርዎን መሥሪያ ቤት የወረዳውን የንግድ ቢሮ ይጠይቁ።

  5. Business licence

    Issued after the steps above; display it and renew it every year.

    የንግድ ፈቃድ

    ከላይ ካሉት ደረጃዎች በኋላ ይሰጣል፤ ይለጥፉት፣ በየዓመቱም ያድሱት።

  6. Records

    Keep every receipt, supplier record and lot number: for tax, for quality and for buyers.

    መዝገቦች

    እያንዳንዱን ደረሰኝ፣ የአቅራቢ መዝገብና የምርት ቁጥር ያስቀምጡ፦ ለግብር፣ ለጥራትና ለገዢዎች።

PART 8Risks · ስጋቶች

What can go wrong, and your answer to each

Every business plan should name its risks honestly. These six are the real ones for honey in Ethiopia today.

ምን ሊበላሽ ይችላል፣ ለእያንዳንዱስ መልስዎ

እያንዳንዱ የንግድ ዕቅድ ስጋቶቹን በታማኝነት መጥቀስ አለበት። እነዚህ ስድስቱ ዛሬ በኢትዮጵያ ለማር እውነተኛዎቹ ናቸው።

Risk · ስጋትWhat you do · የሚወስዱት እርምጃ
Adulterated or watered honeyየተበረዘ ወይም ውሃ የተጨመረበት ማርBuy from known beekeepers, test every delivery, lab-test each season.ከሚታወቁ ንብ አናቢዎች ይግዙ፣ እያንዳንዱን ማስረከቢያ ይለኩ፣ በየወቅቱ በላቦራቶሪ ያስመርምሩ።
Drought or a poor flowering seasonድርቅ ወይም ደካማ የአበባ ወቅትBuy from two or three areas with different flowering times; keep 1–2 months of stock.የአበባ ጊዜያቸው ከሚለያይ ከሁለት ሦስት አካባቢዎች ይግዙ፤ ለ1–2 ወር ክምችት ይያዙ።
Crude honey price jumps (tej makers and smugglers bid it up)የጥሬ ማር ዋጋ መዝለል (ጠጅ ቤቶችና ኮንትሮባንዲስቶች ዋጋ ሲያስወድዱ)Season contracts with beekeepers; supply hives on credit repaid in honey; raise prices with a clear quality story.ከንብ አናቢዎች ጋር የወቅት ውል፤ በማር የሚከፈል የቀፎ ብድር፤ በግልጽ የጥራት ታሪክ ዋጋ መጨመር።
Roads closed by conflictበግጭት የተዘጉ መንገዶችSmall stock, sell locally first, keep two routes and two regions of supply.ትንሽ ክምችት፣ መጀመሪያ በአካባቢው መሸጥ፣ ሁለት መንገዶችና ሁለት የአቅርቦት ክልሎች መያዝ።
Fermentation or spoilageመቦካት ወይም መበላሸትWater ≤ 20%, sealed food-grade jars, cool dry store.ውሃ ≤ 20%፣ የታሸጉ የምግብ ብልቃጦች፣ ቀዝቃዛና ደረቅ መጋዘን።
Cash runs out (beekeepers want cash, shops pay late)ጥሬ ገንዘብ ማለቅ (ንብ አናቢዎች ጥሬ ገንዘብ ሲፈልጉ፣ ሱቆች ዘግይተው ሲከፍሉ)Small batches, deposits from big buyers, a weekly cash sheet.ትንሽ ምርት፣ ከትላልቅ ገዢዎች ቅድመ ክፍያ፣ ሳምንታዊ የጥሬ ገንዘብ ሰነድ።
PART 9Milestones · ምዕራፎች

The first 90 days, then three years

Start small enough that a mistake costs weeks, not years. Each milestone below is something you can check.

የመጀመሪያዎቹ 90 ቀናት፣ ከዚያ ሦስት ዓመታት

ስህተት ዓመታትን ሳይሆን ሳምንታትን ብቻ እንዲያስከፍል በትንሹ ይጀምሩ። ከታች ያለው እያንዳንዱ ምዕራፍ ሊያረጋግጡት የሚችሉት ነገር ነው።

Days 1–30ቀን 1–30
  • Visit 10 beekeepers and 10 buyers; agree prices.
  • Build the solar wax melter; order the tank and refractometer.
  • Process and sell a 20 kg trial batch.
  • 10 ንብ አናቢዎችንና 10 ገዢዎችን ይጎብኙ፤ በዋጋ ይስማሙ።
  • የፀሐይ ሰም ማቅለጫውን ይገንቡ፤ ታንኩንና ሪፍራክቶሜትሩን ያዝዙ።
  • 20 ኪግ የሙከራ ምርት አቀነባብረው ይሽጡ።
Days 31–60ቀን 31–60
  • Register on e-Trade; rent and prepare the room.
  • Apply for the certificate of competence.
  • Design the label; open Telegram and TikTok pages.
  • በኢ-ትሬድ ይመዝገቡ፤ ክፍሉን ተከራይተው ያዘጋጁ።
  • የብቃት ማረጋገጫ ያመልክቱ።
  • መለያውን ይንደፉ፤ የቴሌግራምና የቲክቶክ ገጾችን ይክፈቱ።
Days 61–90ቀን 61–90
  • Licence in hand; first 100 kg batch.
  • Five shops and two hotels buying regularly.
  • First wax sale to a soap or candle maker.
  • ፈቃድ በእጅ፤ የመጀመሪያው 100 ኪግ ምርት።
  • አምስት ሱቆችና ሁለት ሆቴሎች በቋሚነት ይገዛሉ።
  • የመጀመሪያ የሰም ሽያጭ ለሳሙና ወይም ለሻማ አምራች።
Month 6ወር 6
  • Above break-even: 150+ kg a month.
  • First lab test report.
  • One helper hired.
  • ከመመጣጠኛ ነጥብ በላይ፦ በወር ከ150 ኪግ በላይ።
  • የመጀመሪያ የላቦራቶሪ ሪፖርት።
  • አንድ ረዳት ተቀጥሯል።
Month 12ወር 12
  • 200–300 kg a month; first hive prototypes tested with 5 beekeepers.
  • Products in Addis Ababa shops.
  • በወር 200–300 ኪግ፤ የመጀመሪያ የቀፎ ሞዴሎች ከ5 ንብ አናቢዎች ጋር ተሞክረዋል።
  • ምርቶች በአዲስ አበባ ሱቆች።
Year 3ዓመት 3
  • 800 kg a month, 300 hives placed, foundation sheets and sensors.
  • Explore organic group certification and an export partner.
  • በወር 800 ኪግ፣ 300 ቀፎዎች ተሰራጭተዋል፣ የሰም ዳብና ዳሳሾች።
  • የቡድን ኦርጋኒክ ሰርተፊኬትና የኤክስፖርት አጋር ማሰስ።

  • Water % of every lot · የእያንዳንዱ ምርት የውሃ %

  • Repeat customers · ተመላሽ ደንበኞች
  • Active supplier beekeepers · ንቁ አቅራቢ ንብ አናቢዎች
  • Cash on hand at the end of the week · በሳምንቱ መጨረሻ ያለ ጥሬ ገንዘብ
