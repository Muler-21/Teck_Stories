PART 1The value staircase · የእሴት መሰላል

The same resource can earn two to ten times more

Ethiopia earned a record amount from exports in 2025/26, but most of it came from two products sold almost raw: gold and coffee. Honey, hides, opal and minerals follow the same pattern. They leave the farm or the mine at the lowest price, and someone else does the cleaning, processing, branding and selling.

Each of those missing steps is a business. This lesson teaches you to see the steps, understand the science that makes each one possible, and build the first one with what you have.

The rule of this lesson: every fact is real and sourced below. Prices are from 2025–2026 and change quickly with inflation and the exchange rate (about 162 birr per US dollar in November 2026). Always check today's prices in your own market.

PART 2The engineer-entrepreneur method · የመሐንዲስ-ሥራ ፈጣሪ ዘዴ

Seven steps from a problem to a business

An engineer asks how does it work, and how can I make it? An entrepreneur asks who needs it, and what will they pay? You need both questions. The seven steps below work for honey, coffee, leather, minerals or energy.

Three formulas every starter needs

1. Margin per unit

margin = price − (raw material + energy + packaging + transport + labour)

2. Break-even: how many units a month you must sell before you earn anything

break-even units = fixed costs per month ÷ margin per unit

3. Mass balance: what goes in must come out as product, by-product or waste

100 kg crude honey → ≈ 70–80 kg honey + 6–10 kg wax + residue

Weigh your own batches. Your real numbers beat any book.

ከችግር እስከ ንግድ፦ ሰባት ደረጃዎች

መሐንዲስ እንዴት ይሠራል፣ እኔስ እንዴት ልሠራው እችላለሁ? ብሎ ይጠይቃል። ሥራ ፈጣሪ ደግሞ ማን ያስፈልገዋል፣ ስንትስ ይከፍላል? ብሎ ይጠይቃል። ሁለቱም ጥያቄዎች ያስፈልጉዎታል። ከታች ያሉት ሰባት ደረጃዎች ለማር፣ ለቡና፣ ለቆዳ፣ ለማዕድን ወይም ለኃይል ይሠራሉ።

እያንዳንዱ ጀማሪ የሚያስፈልገው ሦስት ቀመር

1. በአንድ ምርት የሚገኝ ትርፍ (ማርጂን)

ማርጂን = ዋጋ − (ጥሬ ዕቃ + ኃይል + ማሸጊያ + ትራንስፖርት + ጉልበት)

2. የመመጣጠኛ ነጥብ፦ ትርፍ ከማግኘትዎ በፊት በወር ምን ያህል መሸጥ እንዳለብዎ

የመመጣጠኛ ብዛት = ቋሚ ወርሃዊ ወጪ ÷ በአንድ ምርት ማርጂን

3. የክብደት ሚዛን፦ የገባው ሁሉ እንደ ምርት፣ ተረፈ ምርት ወይም ቆሻሻ መውጣት አለበት

100 ኪግ ጥሬ ማር → ≈ 70–80 ኪግ ማር + 6–10 ኪግ ሰም + ዝቃጭ

የራስዎን ምርት ይመዝኑ። የእርስዎ እውነተኛ ቁጥሮች ከማንኛውም መጽሐፍ ይበልጣሉ።

  1. Find the leak

    Where is value lost? What is thrown away, sold cheap, or imported even though we have the raw material?

    የሚያፈስበትን ይፈልጉ

    እሴት የሚጠፋው የት ነው? ምን ይጣላል፣ በርካሽ ይሸጣል፣ ወይም ጥሬ ዕቃው እያለን ከውጭ ይገባል?

  2. Measure it

    How much, how often, at what price and quality? A scale, a notebook and a phone are enough to start.

    ይለኩት

    ምን ያህል፣ በየስንት ጊዜ፣ በምን ዋጋና ጥራት? ለመጀመር ሚዛን፣ ማስታወሻ ደብተርና ስልክ በቂ ናቸው።

  3. Learn the science

    What is the material made of? What changes it, and what ruins it? This decides your process.

    ሳይንሱን ይማሩ

    ዕቃው ከምን ተሠራ? ምን ይለውጠዋል፣ ምን ያበላሸዋል? ይህ ሂደትዎን ይወስናል።

  4. Build the smallest working version

    Use local wood, metal, glass and scrap. Make it work before you make it pretty.

    ትንሹን የሚሠራ ሞዴል ይገንቡ

    የአገር ውስጥ እንጨት፣ ብረት፣ መስታወትና ቁርጥራጭ ይጠቀሙ። ከማሳመርዎ በፊት እንዲሠራ ያድርጉት።

  5. Test against a standard

    Ethiopian standards, Codex or ISO tell buyers what "good" means. Quality you can prove sets your price.

    ከደረጃ ጋር ያወዳድሩ

    የኢትዮጵያ ደረጃዎች፣ ኮዴክስ ወይም ISO «ጥሩ» ማለት ምን እንደሆነ ለገዢ ይነግራሉ። ማረጋገጥ የሚችሉት ጥራት ዋጋዎን ይወስናል።

  6. Sell a small batch

    Sell 20 to 50 units. Ask buyers what they would change. Fix it, then sell again.

    ትንሽ ምርት ይሽጡ

    ከ20 እስከ 50 ምርቶችን ይሽጡ። ገዢዎችን ምን እንደሚቀይሩ ይጠይቁ። ያስተካክሉ፣ ከዚያ እንደገና ይሽጡ።

  7. Grow with others

    Register the business, join or form a cooperative, find steady suppliers, and borrow only against proven sales.

    ከሌሎች ጋር ያድጉ

    ንግዱን ያስመዝግቡ፣ ማኅበር ይቀላቀሉ ወይም ያቋቁሙ፣ ቋሚ አቅራቢዎችን ያግኙ፣ የተረጋገጠ ሽያጭ ሲኖር ብቻ ይበደሩ።

PART 4Honey and beeswax · ማርና ሰም

Honey: Africa's biggest hive, barely exported

True story

In 2003, Beza Mar Agro-Industry set up a honey processing plant in Adama. It became the first Ethiopian honey company with organic certification and also holds ISO 22000, and it sells to Europe, the Middle East, Japan and the USA. In 2012, Sheka-Nordic started in the forests of the south-west with its own wood workshop that builds transitional and modern hives, plus a processing plant, aiming at 500 tonnes of organic honey and 50 tonnes of wax a year.

Ethiopia produces roughly 45,000 to 55,000 tonnes of honey a year, more than any other African country, and about 5,000 tonnes of wax. Experts put the potential at 500,000 tonnes of honey and 50,000 tonnes of wax. Less than 2% of the honey is exported, and most hives are still traditional: they give 5–8 kg a year, while a modern hive gives about 20 kg of better-quality honey.

The science
  • Honey is about 80% sugar (mostly fructose and glucose) and 17–20% water. Above about 20% water, natural yeasts start to ferment it. That is why the international Codex standard allows at most 20% moisture.
  • Heat damages honey. Warming turns sugars into HMF (hydroxymethylfurfural). Codex allows at most 40 mg/kg (80 for tropical honey). Keep honey below about 40 °C at every step.
  • Crystals are normal. Glucose slowly forms crystals. Crystallised honey is not "sugar added".
  • Wax floats. Beeswax melts at about 62–64 °C and is lighter than water (density about 0.96). Melt it with water, let it settle, and clean wax rises above the dirt.
Build it

1. Solar wax melter. An insulated wooden box, a black metal tray, a double-glass lid, tilted to face the sun. On a sunny day the inside reaches 70–90 °C. Old comb melts, runs through a mesh and sets in a mould. No fuel needed.

2. Settling tank. A food-grade bucket or a stainless tank with a tap at the bottom. Strain the honey through a coarse, then a fine mesh, and let it stand 2–3 days at room temperature. Bubbles and wax rise; draw clear honey from the tap.

3. Moisture check. A honey refractometer reads the water content from one drop. Above 20%: do not sell as table honey.

Your mission
  1. Visit five beekeepers. Record hive type, kg per hive, price, and what they do with wax.
  2. Buy 20 kg of crude honey. Strain, settle, weigh the honey and the wax: that is your own mass balance.
  3. Pack 40 jars, label them with the place and harvest month, sell them, and calculate your margin.

PART 5Coffee · ቡና

Coffee: the birthplace still sells it green

Coffee earned a record $3 billion in 2025/26, close to 30% of all export earnings. Production is forecast at a record 11.56 million 60-kg bags (about 694,000 tonnes), and Ethiopians drink about a third of it themselves. Yet green beans are 99.5% of exports. Roasted exports are small but tripled in 2023/24 to about 27,000 bags, and a reform now lets local roasters export roasted coffee for foreign currency.

True story

Bethlehem Tilahun Alemu, the founder of soleRebels (Part 6), went on to build a second brand, Garden of Coffee, built on Ethiopian coffee. The pattern is the one this lesson teaches: keep the last steps, the roasting, branding and selling, in the country.

The science
  • Green coffee holds about 10–12% water.
  • Drying (to about 150 °C): water leaves and the bean turns yellow.
  • Browning: sugars and amino acids react (Maillard reactions) and sugars caramelise. Together they make hundreds of aroma compounds.
  • First crack at about 196 °C: steam and CO₂ crack the bean open. Light to medium roasts finish soon after.
  • Weight loss: beans lose 12–20% of their weight, mostly water. So 1 kg of roasted coffee needs about 1.2 kg of green coffee.
  • Degassing: roasted beans release CO₂ for days. That is why good bags have a one-way valve.
Build it

A 1–2 kg drum roaster. A perforated steel drum turned at 30–60 rpm by a geared motor (or a hand crank), over a gas burner. Put a K-type thermocouple in the bean mass, read it with an Arduino or ESP32 through a MAX6675 board, and log the temperature every 10 seconds. That is a roast curve: the record that lets you repeat a good roast. Dump into a cooling tray with a fan and cool within 4 minutes.

Safety and the law

Chaff (the papery skin) can catch fire. Roast with good ventilation, keep a fire blanket close, and never leave a hot roaster alone.

Your mission
  1. Buy 5 kg each of three origins (for example Sidama, Guji, Jimma).
  2. Roast each light, medium and dark. Log every curve.
  3. Hold a tasting with 10 people. Choose a profile, price it, and sell 30 bags.

PART 6Hides and leather · ቆዳና ሌጦ

Leather: Africa's largest herd, few finished goods

True story

In 2004–05, fresh out of college, Bethlehem Tilahun Alemu started soleRebels with five workers in a workshop on her grandmother's plot in Zenebework, Addis Ababa. The shoes used soles from recycled car tyres and local materials. soleRebels grew into a global brand selling in over 45 countries, and in 2014 she launched a second company, Republic of Leather. Her rule, in her words: produce locally and sell globally.

Ethiopia has an estimated 66–70 million cattle, and Bati and Selale skins are among the finest leathers in the world. But many hides are damaged by flaying cuts and poor curing, there is little grading or price reward for quality, and tanneries have closed for pollution. Most tanneries are now in Modjo, where a Leather City with shared waste-water treatment is planned and young people have been trained to make bags, belts and shoes.

The science
  • Skin is mostly collagen, a protein made of long fibres.
  • Bacteria start rotting a hide within hours in warm weather. Salting or drying removes the water they need. A hide must be cured on the day of slaughter.
  • Tanning links collagen fibres together so the skin stops rotting and stays flexible. Chrome tanning uses chromium(III) salts: fast, but its waste water must be treated, because chromium(III) can turn into toxic chromium(VI). Vegetable tanning uses tannins from bark and pods (such as acacia): slower, weeks instead of days, but simpler for small workshops.
Build it

A leather-goods bench. Buy finished leather from a tannery. Tools: a cutting mat, a sharp knife, a skiving knife to thin edges, an edge beveller, an awl, two needles and waxed thread (wax it with your own beeswax from Part 4), and a wooden stitching clamp. Make patterns from cardboard. Start with a belt, then a wallet, then a bag.

A hide-quality service. Teach butchers clean flaying, salt hides the same day, and sell graded hides. Better hides fetch better prices.

Your mission
  1. Make 10 belts. Time each one.
  2. Cost = leather + buckle + thread + your hours × your hourly rate.
  3. Sell them, ask buyers what to change, and make the next 10 faster.

The large deposits are Lega Dembi and Tulu Kapi in Oromia and Kurmuk in Benishangul. A domestic refinery is coming online, and the National Bank plans to leave the gold market around the end of 2026 so that other banks can buy.

Opal was found near Wegel Tena in Wollo in 2008. It is hydrophane: it turns transparent when it soaks up water. Miners sell most of it rough, far below the price of polished stones.

Lithium and tantalum come from Kenticha in Guji: an ore body of 87.7 million tonnes at 0.78% lithium oxide, with a dense-media separation plant. Salt: Afdera, Dobe and Berhale in Afar supply about 92% of the country's salt, and the law requires it to be iodised.

The science
  • Density separates minerals. Gold weighs 19.3 g/cm³, quartz only 2.65: gold is about 7 times heavier. Panning, sluice boxes, shaking tables and centrifugal concentrators use that difference. No mercury needed.
  • Spodumene (LiAlSi₂O₆, about 3.15 g/cm³) sinks in a heavy liquid that quartz and feldspar (about 2.6) float on. That is dense-media separation, the method at Kenticha.
  • Opal is silica with water (SiO₂·nH₂O). Tiny silica spheres stacked in rows split light into colours: "play of colour". Hardness about 5.5–6.5, so it is cut with diamond wheels and polished with cerium oxide.
  • Salt from brine: as brine evaporates, the least soluble salts come out first: gypsum, then table salt (NaCl), then potassium and magnesium salts. That order is why the Danakil holds potash beds.
Build it

A small opal cabbing machine. A motor, a shaft, diamond wheels from coarse to fine (about 220, 600, 1200 grit), a water drip to keep stone and dust wet, and a felt pad with cerium oxide to polish. Cut opal wet, and let hydrophane stones dry slowly.

Safety and the law
  • Mercury: burning gold–mercury amalgam releases vapour that damages nerves and kidneys. Gravity methods avoid it.
  • Cyanide is used at industrial gold plants; rights monitors have called the area around Lega Dembi one of Africa's most polluted sites.
  • Dust: grinding stone dry can cause silicosis. Always cut wet.
  • Licences: mining needs a licence from the regional mining bureau, and gold must be sold through legal channels. Never buy from sites run by armed groups.

Energy: a giant dam, and half the country still in the dark

The Grand Ethiopian Renaissance Dam (GERD), 5,150 MW, was inaugurated on 9 September 2025 and should produce about 15,700 GWh a year. Yet some 45% of Ethiopians have no electricity. Sunshine averages 5.5–6.5 kWh per square metre per day. The Rift Valley holds an estimated 10,000 MW of geothermal heat: Aluto-Langano opened in 1998 and is being rehabilitated, and the Tulu Moye and Corbetti projects are restarting. Gas from Calub and Hilala will feed a 3-million-tonne urea fertilizer plant at Gode, expected by 2029; Ethiopia now uses about 1.7 million tonnes of fertilizer a year.

True story

Abel, a young Ethiopian entrepreneur, started Green Hope to make solar affordable by building combiner boxes (the boxes that safely join panels) locally instead of importing them. By 2025 it had 18 people, a contract for solar street lights in Addis Ababa's CMC district, and clients running solar water pumps of up to 45 kW.

The science

Power and energy are different. Power (watts, W) is how fast energy is used. Energy (watt-hours, Wh) is power × time. A 10 W lamp on for 5 hours uses 50 Wh.

Size a solar kit in three lines:

daily energy = Σ (watts × hours)
panel watts = daily energy ÷ (sun hours × 0.75)
battery Wh = daily energy × days of backup ÷ usable fraction

Example: a village kiosk. 4 LED lamps × 9 W × 5 h = 180 Wh; charging 10 phones × 15 Wh = 150 Wh; a 20 W radio and fan × 6 h = 120 Wh. Total 450 Wh a day. Panel: 450 ÷ (5.5 × 0.75) ≈ 109 W, so use 120–150 W. Battery (lithium iron phosphate, 80% usable, one day): 450 ÷ 0.8 ≈ 560 Wh, so a 12 V 50 Ah battery (600 Wh).

Gas to fertilizer: CH₄ + H₂O → CO + 3H₂, then N₂ + 3H₂ → 2NH₃ (Haber–Bosch), then 2NH₃ + CO₂ → CO(NH₂)₂ + H₂O: urea.

ምሳሌ፦ የገጠር ሱቅ። 4 የLED መብራቶች × 9 W × 5 ሰዓት = 180 Wh፤ 10 ስልኮችን መሙላት × 15 Wh = 150 Wh፤ 20 W ሬዲዮና ማራገቢያ × 6 ሰዓት = 120 Wh። ድምር በቀን 450 Wh። ፓነል፦ 450 ÷ (5.5 × 0.75) ≈ 109 W፣ ስለዚህ 120–150 W ይጠቀሙ። ባትሪ (ሊቲየም አይረን ፎስፌት፣ 80% ጥቅም ላይ የሚውል፣ ለአንድ ቀን)፦ 450 ÷ 0.8 ≈ 560 Wh፣ ስለዚህ 12 V 50 Ah ባትሪ (600 Wh)።

ጋዝ ወደ ማዳበሪያ፦ CH₄ + H₂O → CO + 3H₂፣ ከዚያ N₂ + 3H₂ → 2NH₃ (ሃበር–ቦሽ)፣ ከዚያ 2NH₃ + CO₂ → CO(NH₂)₂ + H₂O፦ ዩሪያ።

PART 9Safety, law and fairness · ደህንነት፣ ሕግና ፍትሐዊነት

Build a business that lasts

Safety and the law
  • Register. Commercial registration and business licences are now done online on the e-Trade portal (etrade.gov.et). You need a TIN, a trade name and premises; licences are renewed every year. Regulated sectors such as food and mining also need a certificate of competence from the sector office.
  • Food rules. Honey and coffee are food: clean premises, food-grade containers, labels with the product, place, weight and date.
  • Chemicals. Mercury, cyanide and chromium harm workers and rivers. Choose methods that avoid them, and treat waste water.
  • Fair work. No child labour. Pay suppliers on time; fair prices make loyal suppliers.
  • Security. In November 2026, armed conflict affects parts of Amhara, Tigray and Afar, and roads such as Dessie–Woldia are contested. Check the situation before you travel or sign supply deals, and keep stock small where roads are unsafe.

PART 10Your path · የእርስዎ መንገድ

Start this month, with what you have

You do not need a factory to start. You need one resource, one problem, a notebook and the courage to sell a small batch. Here is a plan for the first six months, and a quiz to check what you learned.

ባለዎት ነገር በዚህ ወር ይጀምሩ

ለመጀመር ፋብሪካ አያስፈልግዎትም። አንድ ሀብት፣ አንድ ችግር፣ ማስታወሻ ደብተርና ትንሽ ምርት ለመሸጥ ድፍረት ያስፈልግዎታል። የመጀመሪያዎቹ ስድስት ወራት ዕቅድና የተማሩትን የሚፈትሽ ጥያቄዎች ይኸውና።

Weeks 1–2

Pick one resource near you. Visit producers and buyers. Measure quantities and prices.

ሳምንት 1–2፦ በአቅራቢያዎ አንድ ሀብት ይምረጡ። አምራቾችንና ገዢዎችን ይጎብኙ። መጠንና ዋጋ ይለኩ።

Weeks 3–4

Learn the science. Build your first tool: a wax melter, a roaster, a cutting bench or a solar kit.

ሳምንት 3–4፦ ሳይንሱን ይማሩ። የመጀመሪያ መሣሪያዎን ይገንቡ፦ ሰም ማቅለጫ፣ ቆይ፣ መቁረጫ ጠረጴዛ ወይም የፀሐይ ኪት።

Weeks 5–8

Make and sell a first batch of 20–50 units. Record every cost. Calculate your margin and break-even.

ሳምንት 5–8፦ ከ20–50 የሚሆኑ የመጀመሪያ ምርቶችን ሠርተው ይሽጡ። እያንዳንዱን ወጪ ይመዝግቡ። ማርጂንና የመመጣጠኛ ነጥብ ያስሉ።

Month 3

Register on e-Trade. Write a one-page plan (see Part 3: the business plan).

ወር 3፦ በኢ-ትሬድ ይመዝገቡ። የአንድ ገጽ ዕቅድ ይጻፉ (ክፍል 3፦ የንግድ ዕቅዱን ይመልከቱ)።

Month 6

Steady suppliers, a second product from your by-product, and a partner or cooperative.

ወር 6፦ ቋሚ አቅራቢዎች፣ ከተረፈ ምርትዎ ሁለተኛ ምርት፣ እና አጋር ወይም ማኅበር።

Quiz · ጥያቄዎች

1. Why must table honey stay below about 20% water? የገበታ ማር ውሃው ከ20% በታች መሆን ያለበት ለምንድን ነው?

Above that, natural yeasts ferment it.

ከዚያ በላይ ሲሆን የተፈጥሮ እርሾዎች ያቦኩታል።

2. Why should you never heat honey above about 40 °C? ማርን ከ40 °C በላይ ለምን አያሞቁትም?

Heat turns sugars into HMF, a quality marker with a legal limit.

ሙቀት ስኳሩን ወደ HMF ይቀይራል፤ ይህ ሕጋዊ ገደብ ያለው የጥራት መለኪያ ነው።

3. How can you separate wax from dirt without chemicals? ሰምን ከቆሻሻ ያለ ኬሚካል እንዴት ይለያሉ?

Melt it with water; wax (density about 0.96) floats.

ከውሃ ጋር ያቅልጡት፤ ሰም (ጥግግቱ 0.96 ገደማ) ይንሳፈፋል።

4. How much green coffee makes 1 kg of roasted coffee? 1 ኪግ የተቆላ ቡና ስንት ያልተቆላ ቡና ይፈልጋል?

About 1.2 kg, because roasting removes 12–20% of the weight.

1.2 ኪግ ገደማ፤ መቁላት ከክብደቱ 12–20% ስለሚቀንስ።

5. What does tanning do to skin? ማልፋት በቆዳ ላይ ምን ያደርጋል?

It links collagen fibres so the skin stops rotting and stays flexible.

የኮላጅን ፋይበሮችን ያስተሳስራል፤ ስለዚህ ቆዳው አይበሰብስም፣ ተጣጣፊም ይሆናል።

6. How can gold be concentrated without mercury? ወርቅን ያለ ሜርኩሪ እንዴት መሰብሰብ ይቻላል?

By density: gold is about 7 times heavier than quartz (panning, sluices, shaking tables).

በጥግግት፦ ወርቅ ከኳርትዝ 7 እጥፍ ገደማ ይከብዳል (ማጠብ፣ የውሃ ቦይ፣ የሚንቀጠቀጥ ጠረጴዛ)።

7. A kiosk uses 450 Wh a day with 5.5 sun hours. About what panel size? አንድ ሱቅ በቀን 450 Wh ይጠቀማል፤ የፀሐይ ሰዓቱ 5.5 ነው። ስንት ዋት ፓነል ያስፈልገዋል?

450 ÷ (5.5 × 0.75) ≈ 109 W, so 120–150 W.

450 ÷ (5.5 × 0.75) ≈ 109 W፣ ስለዚህ 120–150 W።

8. What is a break-even point? የመመጣጠኛ ነጥብ ምንድን ነው?

Fixed monthly costs ÷ margin per unit: the sales you need before you earn a profit.
